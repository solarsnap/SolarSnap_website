EcoFlow STREAM Is Now Certified for UK Self-Install — What to Buy and What to Avoid

30 September 2026 · 7 min read

This page contains affiliate links. If you purchase through these links, SolarSnap may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

EcoFlow has been the UK government's named technology partner for the plug-in solar rollout since March 2026. On 22 September, their battery-free STREAM plug-in solar kits finally achieved what everyone had been waiting for: Compliant status on the ENA G98 Type Test Register.

That's genuinely significant. EcoFlow is one of the most recognised brands in the portable power space, with established UK support, a polished monitoring app, and prices starting at £449. For many buyers, an EcoFlow kit will be the natural first choice.

But there's a catch that could cost you real money if you miss it: EcoFlow makes multiple STREAM variants, and several of them are not certified for self-install. Buying the wrong one means you still need an electrician. This guide tells you exactly which kits are compliant, which aren't, and what to look for when you buy.

The Two Certified EcoFlow Kits

Two EcoFlow entries are currently listed as Compliant on the ENA register, both published on 22 September 2026:

1. EcoFlow STREAM Plug-In Solar Kits — Roof and Facade Mounts

ENA reference: ECOFL/21128/V1/A3
Covers: Flat roof kit, Facade (wall) kit, Pitched roof kit
Prices: £449 (Facade 800W) to £699 (Pitched roof 1040W)
Status: Compliant ✅

This is the entry point — two panels and the STREAM 800W microinverter, with mounting hardware suited to your installation type. The cheapest compliant EcoFlow kit is the STREAM Adjustable Facade Kit 800W at £449, which comes with two 400W panels on adjustable wall brackets. It's also the most straightforward to install for anyone with a south or southeast-facing wall or fence.

The full facade range by panel size:

  • Facade 800W (2 × 400W panels) — £449
  • Facade 900W (2 × 450W panels) — £499
  • Facade 1040W (2 × 520W panels) — £549

Flat roof and pitched roof variants follow the same panel configurations at slightly higher prices (£549–£699) to account for the different mounting hardware.

2. EcoFlow EF-PS-800 + SPDG505 Panel Kit

ENA reference: ECOFL/21606/V1/A1
Status: Compliant ✅

A second compliant entry pairing the EF-PS-800 microinverter with SPDG505 panels. Pricing and availability on this variant are still being confirmed — check EcoFlow's UK store directly for current status.

What's NOT Certified for Self-Install — Read This Carefully

This is where buyers can get caught out. EcoFlow's STREAM range includes several models that are battery-integrated — and battery kits are explicitly excluded from SI 2026/848, the law that permits self-install via a standard socket.

These STREAM variants are not certified for the plug-in socket route and still require a qualified electrician for installation:

  • STREAM Pro (~£799) — integrated battery, hardwired installation only
  • STREAM Ultra (~£899) — integrated battery, hardwired installation only
  • STREAM Ultra X (~£1,399) — integrated battery, hardwired installation only
  • STREAM 5000 / STREAM 3000 (~£1,599) — large battery system, hardwired installation only
  • STREAM AC Pro — battery storage add-on, hardwired installation only

These are excellent products for households who want battery storage and are happy to use an electrician — but they are not self-install plug-in solar under the current regulations. The law specifically covers panels-plus-microinverter with no battery, full stop.

One more important note: as of 22 September, EcoFlow's own website still displayed confusing messaging on some product pages. Always cross-check the ENA register reference (ECOFL/21128/V1/A3 or ECOFL/21606/V1/A1) before buying to confirm your specific kit is compliant.

What's Missing — The Balcony and Ground Mount Gap

There's one notable gap in EcoFlow's certified range that affects a significant number of potential buyers: no balcony or ground mount is currently in the compliant register entry.

The three certified mount types are flat roof, facade (wall), and pitched roof. If you were planning to clip panels to a balcony railing — one of the most common setups for flat and rented properties — that specific configuration isn't in EcoFlow's current ENA entry. A balcony or ground mount kit from EcoFlow can still be installed via the hardwired electrician route, but not yet via the self-install socket route.

Whether EcoFlow adds balcony and ground mount variants to the register is a key development to watch. In the meantime, UKSOL and PatioSun both have certified kits with balcony and ground mount options.

EcoFlow's competitive position is strong — the £449 facade kit matches PatioSun Lite on price while offering a significantly more established brand, better warranty, and a polished monitoring app. The key question is whether the facade mount suits your specific outdoor space.

Why the Monitoring App Matters

One genuine advantage EcoFlow brings over smaller competitors is their app ecosystem. The EcoFlow app shows real-time generation data, integrates with smart plugs to automatically run appliances when your panel is generating, and provides historical output data. For someone who wants to actively optimise their solar usage rather than just leave it plugged in, this is a meaningful differentiator.

The monitoring app also makes it easy to verify your system is working correctly after installation — something that's harder to assess without data when you're self-installing for the first time.

The Expected Annual Savings

Based on current electricity prices of 26.32p/kWh (from 1 October 2026) and typical UK conditions, a well-positioned EcoFlow STREAM 800W kit can save approximately £100–£175 per year — at the higher end for south-facing locations in southern England with good daytime self-consumption, and towards the lower end for more northerly locations or homes empty during the day.

At £449 for the entry kit, that gives a payback period of roughly 3–5 years in favourable conditions. The panels carry a 25-year warranty, so the long-term financial case is straightforward if the placement is good.

Before You Buy — Check Your Placement First

All of this is academic if your specific outdoor space isn't well-suited for solar. A facade kit pointing east rather than south, or a wall position with afternoon shade from a neighbouring building, will generate significantly less than the headline figures suggest.

Quick Checklist — EcoFlow STREAM Self-Install

  • ✅ Check ENA register for ECOFL/21128/V1/A3 or ECOFL/21606/V1/A1 before buying
  • ✅ Facade, flat roof or pitched roof mount — currently the only compliant EcoFlow mount types
  • ✅ No battery in the kit — battery-integrated STREAM variants require an electrician
  • ✅ Check your consumer unit has a Type A, F or B RCD — not Type AC
  • ✅ You need an outdoor weatherproof socket — no extension leads permitted
  • ✅ Submit G98 notification to your DNO within 28 days of connecting
  • ⚠️ Balcony and ground mounts not yet in EcoFlow's compliant register entry
  • ⚠️ One device per household — confirmed in G98 August 2026 amendment
