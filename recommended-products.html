EcoFlow PowerStream Balcony Solar System

EcoFlow's balcony solar system is one of the most complete plug-in solar kits available in the UK. The PowerStream microinverter is a standout feature — it intelligently manages power flow between your panels, your home circuit, and an optional battery, so you can start without storage and add it later. The companion app gives you real-time generation data, which is genuinely useful for understanding your usage patterns.

The panels are monocrystalline, good quality, and the mounting hardware covers most scenarios — balcony rails, walls, and ground mounts. Build quality feels solid. EcoFlow's UK customer support and warranty process are generally well-regarded.

Best for: Homes with south- or east/west-facing outdoor space, especially if you want the option to add battery storage later. Also worth considering if you're likely to want to monitor and optimise your usage over time.

EcoFlow
EcoFlow DELTA 2 + Solar Panel Bundle

If your home is empty during the day and you want to store solar energy for evening use, the DELTA 2 bundle offers an alternative approach: a portable power station with integrated solar charging. Unlike a standard plug-in system, the DELTA 2 charges from a panel during the day and then discharges through its AC outlets in the evening.

It's less efficient than a grid-tied balcony system (some energy is lost in the battery conversion cycle), but it's genuinely portable and doesn't require any connection to your home's ring main. Worth considering for renters who want flexibility, or as a first step before committing to a fixed installation.

Best for: Renters or flat dwellers with no access to a fixed outdoor socket. Households where daytime electricity use is very low and evening storage is the priority. Those who want a portable system they can use for camping or outdoor events as well.
