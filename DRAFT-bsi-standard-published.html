What Has Just Changed

Until today, plug-in solar panels existed in a regulatory gap in the UK. The wiring regulations (BS 7671 Amendment 4) had been updated in April 2026 to accommodate plug-in solar — but without a product standard, no individual kit could be certified for fully compliant DIY self-installation. The safest route was to have a qualified electrician make the final connection.

That changes today. The publication of [BSI STANDARD NUMBER] means that kits certified to this standard can now be legally self-installed by any UK householder — no electrician required. You buy the kit, follow the instructions, plug it in. That's it.

This brings the UK fully in line with Germany, France, and the rest of Europe where plug-in solar has been mainstream for years.

What About Kits Already Installed?

If you already have a plug-in solar system installed — either self-installed or via an electrician — you don't need to do anything. The new standard applies to new installations going forward. Your existing G98 notification remains valid.

If you self-installed a kit before today without an electrician, it's worth reviewing whether the kit you used would meet the new standard's requirements. Kits from reputable brands with CE-marked, anti-islanding protected microinverters are very likely to meet the specification — but if you have any doubt, a quick check by a qualified electrician will confirm your installation is compliant.

What About the G98 Notification?

This hasn't changed. You still need to notify your Distribution Network Operator (DNO) within 28 days of installation. Most reputable kit suppliers handle this for you, or you can submit it yourself at energynetworks.org. It's a straightforward notification, not an approval process.

What This Means for Renters

The new standard strengthens the case for renters considerably. A kit certified to a published British Standard is a much stronger basis for a landlord request under the Renters' Rights Act 2025 — the kit's compliance is no longer a matter of interpretation, it's documented.
