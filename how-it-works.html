How SolarSnap works

Four simple steps — all from your phone. No technical knowledge, no surveyor, no waiting.

1
Free

Open the app and allow location access

Download SolarSnap free on iOS or Android. When you first open it, allow location access — this pulls accurate solar irradiance data for your exact position across Europe. Your location is never shared.

  • Free to download
  • No account required for your first assessment
  • Works anywhere across Europe
↓
2
Point & Assess

Stand at your location and point at the sky

Walk to where you plan to install your solar panel. Hold your phone toward the sky in the direction and angle the panel would face, then tap Assess. The app reads your compass bearing and tilt in real time.

  • Live facing direction and tilt readout
  • Simple on-screen guidance
  • Takes just a few seconds
↓
3
Free result

Get your instant suitability verdict

Within seconds you'll receive a clear rating — Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor — along with a sky obstruction breakdown and your panel orientation. The free result tells you everything you need to know about whether the location is worth pursuing.

  • Excellent / Good / Fair / Poor rating
  • Sky obstruction analysis (% sky vs obstructed)
  • Panel orientation — facing direction and tilt
  • Sun position in real time
↓
4
Premium

Unlock the full yield report

Want the numbers? Upgrade to Premium with a one-off purchase to unlock the detailed yield report — estimated annual energy output in kWh, potential annual savings in £, and a month-by-month energy breakdown based on real EU solar irradiance data.

  • Estimated annual energy yield (kWh)
  • Estimated annual saving (£)
  • Monthly energy breakdown chart
  • Adjust panel capacity and electricity price
